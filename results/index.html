Results & Case Outcomes

Outcomes We Pursue for New Jersey Clients

Every employment matter is different — but every strategy should have a clear goal.

Free Consultation

Employment cases resolve in many ways: negotiated settlements, severance renegotiations, agency resolutions, and — when necessary — trial verdicts. At Sleemi Law, the first conversation is about what outcome actually fixes your problem.

For Employees

  • Financial recovery — lost wages, benefits, and damages for discrimination, retaliation, and wage violations
  • Severance improvements — more money, better references, and removal of overreaching non-competes
  • Reinstatement or reference repair — the right outcome isn't always monetary
  • Wage recovery — unpaid overtime and wages, including doubled liquidated damages where the law allows

For Businesses

  • Early resolution — claims and agency charges addressed before they become lawsuits
  • Compliance fixes — policies, handbooks, and contracts drafted to prevent repeat disputes
  • Defense — vigorous, cost-aware representation when claims are contested
