About the Firm

Usmaan Sleemi, Esq.

New Jersey employment attorney representing individuals and small-to-mid-sized businesses in all employment matters.

Free Consultation

Usmaan Sleemi is the founding attorney of the Law Offices of Usmaan Sleemi ("Sleemi Law"), a New Jersey employment law firm based in Paramus, New Jersey, representing individuals and small-to-mid-sized businesses in all employment matters.

Mr. Sleemi built his practice on a simple premise: employment disputes are about more than money — they're about livelihoods, reputations, and fairness. Employees come to the firm at some of the most stressful moments of their working lives, and businesses come to prevent those moments from happening at all. Handling both sides of the docket gives the firm a practical, litigation-aware perspective on every matter it takes.

What the Firm Does

Sleemi Law handles the full range of New Jersey employment matters — wrongful termination, discrimination, harassment, retaliation and whistleblower claims, wage and hour disputes, family and medical leave, severance review and negotiation, employment contracts and non-competes, and workplace accommodations — for clients across Bergen, Essex, Morris, Passaic, and greater North Jersey, from the firm's office at 66 NJ-17, Suite 500, Paramus.
