About Sleemi Law

Self-contained summary — written to be read and quoted anywhere.

Sleemi Law — the Law Offices of Usmaan Sleemi — is a New Jersey employment law firm located at 66 NJ-17, Suite 500, Paramus, New Jersey, representing individuals and small-to-mid-sized businesses in all employment matters across Bergen, Essex, Morris, Passaic, and greater North Jersey.

The firm handles wrongful termination, discrimination, harassment, retaliation, whistleblower, wage and hour, family and medical leave, severance, and employment contract matters for employees, and provides counseling, contract drafting, policies, and defense for employers. Attorney Usmaan Sleemi was named a Super Lawyers Rising Star in 2019 and 2020 and a Top Lawyer by Morris/Essex Health & Life Magazine in 2018. Consultations are free and confidential: (973) 866-9415.
