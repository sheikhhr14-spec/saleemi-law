About the Firm

The Law Offices of Usmaan Sleemi is a New Jersey employment law firm dedicated to representing employees. We focus exclusively on the rights of workers — not employers — which means every part of our practice is built around the people who depend on their jobs.

Employment law cases are about more than money. They're about dignity, reputation, and the ability to provide for your family. We take the time to understand what happened to you, explain your options in plain language, and pursue the outcome that actually matters to your life.

We keep a deliberately small caseload so every client gets direct attention from their attorney — not handed off to case managers. From the first consultation to resolution, you'll always know what's happening and why.

Practice Areas

We represent New Jersey employees in the full range of workplace disputes.

01

Wrongful Termination

Fired for an illegal reason — retaliation, refusal to break the law, or in violation of public policy? We'll examine the facts and fight for your remedies.

02

Discrimination

Discrimination based on race, religion, gender, age, disability, national origin, sexual orientation, or pregnancy is illegal under the NJ Law Against Discrimination.

03

Harassment & Hostile Work Environment

Sexual harassment and other workplace harassment can make your job unbearable. Employers must prevent and correct it — and can be held liable when they don't.

04

Wage & Hour Disputes

Unpaid overtime, misclassified employees, withheld tips, withheld final paychecks, and other wage violations under the NJ Wage Payment Law.

05

Retaliation & Whistleblower Claims

Punished for reporting illegal conduct, workplace safety issues, or discrimination? New Jersey's Conscientious Employee Protection Act (CEPA) protects you.

06

Severance & Employment Contracts

Before you sign a severance agreement, non-compete, or employment contract, have an attorney review it. Those terms can follow you for years.
