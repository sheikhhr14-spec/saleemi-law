FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How consultations, fees, deadlines, and confidentiality work at Sleemi Law.

Ask Your Question
Does Sleemi Law charge for an initial consultation?
No. The initial consultation is free and confidential. You'll get an honest assessment of your situation and your options before you decide anything.
Do you represent employees or employers?
Both. Sleemi Law represents individuals in employment disputes and advises small and mid-sized businesses on hiring, discipline, contracts, policies, and compliance — experience on both sides of disputes that strengthens our work for each.
How much will my case cost?
It depends on the matter. Many employment claims are handled on a contingency basis, where fees are a percentage of recovery and you owe nothing unless you win. Contract review and business counseling are typically flat-fee or hourly. We explain all costs up front.
How long do I have to file a claim?
Deadlines vary: generally two years for NJLAD discrimination and most retaliation claims, one year for CEPA whistleblower claims, and contract-specific windows for severance and employment agreements. Time is the most common reason valid claims die — don't wait.
Will my employer find out I consulted a lawyer?
Not from us. Consultations are confidential and privileged. Most clients consult us while still employed; you are free to decide what, if anything, to do next.
Do I have to come to the Paramus office?
No. We serve all of New Jersey and can consult by phone or video, with evening and weekend appointments available. In-person meetings are welcome at 66 NJ-17, Suite 500, Paramus, NJ.
I was offered a severance agreement with a short deadline. Can you help fast?
Yes — severance review is one of our most time-sensitive services, and we can often turn a review around quickly. Employees 40 and over have at least 21 days plus a 7-day revocation period by federal law.
What if I'm not sure I have a case?
That's exactly what the free consultation is for. We'll tell you plainly whether you have viable claims, what they're worth, and whether it makes sense to act.

Don't see your question? Contact the firm — the consultation is free and confidential.
